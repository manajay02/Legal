MOHAMMED LAFFAR, J.
I heard the learned Counsel for the Petitioners in support of this application
on 07.08.2024. I heard the learned Deputy Solicitor General for the
Respondents as well.
The Petitioners are seeking a mandate inter alia in the nature of a writ of
Mandamus directing the Respondents to backdate the effective date of their
promotions from 30.03.2018.
The Petitioners have been promoted to the post of Airport Firefighter grade I
from the date setout below.
1st Petitioner  10.12.2021
2nd Petitioner  12.10.2022
3rd Petitioner  12.10.2022

--- Page 5 ---
4th Petitioner  12.10.2022
5th Petitioner  12.10.2022
6th Petitioner  27.10.2022
7th Petitioner  18.11.2022
8th Petitioner  13.10.2022
9th Petitioner  30.07.2022
10th Petitioner  06.07.2022
11th Petitioner  21.04.2021
12th Petitioner  08.08.2022
The contention of the learned counsel for the Petitioners is the effective date
of promotion should be 30.03.2018. The contention of the learned D.S.G. is
that the date of promotion should be the date on which the Petitioners have
obtained heavy vehicle driving license.
Admittedly, in accordance with the scheme of recruitment and promotion
(SOR) which is marked as P6 one of the criteria of eligibility for the promotion
is obtaining Heavy Vehicle Driving license. It is understood that, the
employees those who have not obtained Heavy Vehicle Driving license are not
eligible for the promotion of Airport Firefighter Grade I post.
In this case it is abundantly clear that the date of promotions is as per the
dates on which each Petitioner have obtained their Heavy Vehicle Driving
license. In this premise it appears to this court that the date of promotions as
set out in paragraph 24 of the Petition is strictly in accordance with the SOR
marked as P6. As such, it is the view of this court that the Petition is devoid
of merits.
For the foregoing reasons, the notices are refused, and the application is
dismissed, no costs.
Application dismissed, no costs.
