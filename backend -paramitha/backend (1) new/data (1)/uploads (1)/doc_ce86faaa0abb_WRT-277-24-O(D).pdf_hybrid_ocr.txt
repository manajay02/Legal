MOHAMMED LAFFAR, J.
The Petitioner is seeking, inter alia, to quash the Order marked Y and review
the decision marked X. Moreover, the Petitioner is seeking an Order to
dismiss the application made by the 7th Respondent before the Debt
Conciliation Board.
I heard the learned counsel for the Petitioner in support of this Application. I
heard the learned counsel for the 7th Respondent as well.
Admittedly, the 7th Respondent had executed the transfer deed bearing No.
1501 dated 25.10.2012 attested by Niluka Sithari De Silva, Notary Public in
favour of the Petitioner. Thereafter, the 7th Respondent filed an application
before the Debt Conciliation Board stating that, he has not transferred the
property by virtue of the deed bearing No. 1501, whereas he had executed a
mortgage bond in respect of the loan what he obtained from the petitioner.
At this point, the Debt Conciliation Board directed the 7th Respondent to prove
the possession to the Property. Since the Petitioner and the 7th Respondent
are claiming possession of the subject matter, there was an inquiry held
before the Board in this regard. After the inquiry, the Debt Conciliation Board
on 15.11.2023 held that the 7th Respondent is in possession of the subject
matter. The said Order is marked as X. Thereupon, under Section 54 (1) of
the Debt Conciliation Board Ordinance, the Petitioner made an application to
review the said decision. The 1st to 5th Respondents dismissed that application
which is marked as Y.
The question before this Court is whether the Debt Conciliation Board had
adequate evidence to hold that the 7th Respondent is in possession.

--- Page 3 ---
It is evident that, the 7th Respondent has constructed permeant buildings on
the land in dispute. Moreover, the evidence given by the petitioner before the
Debt Conciliation Board substantiates the position that the Petitioner has no
possession in this land. Furthermore, it is to be noted that after the execution
of the said purported deed of transfer, there is no evidence to substantiate the
contention of the Petitioner that, the vacant possession of the subject matter
had been handed over to the petitioner by the 7th Respondent.
Having scrutinized the evidence adduced before the Board, it is abundantly
clear that, the possession of the 7th Respondent of the property in dispute has
been well established before the Board, and therefore I dont see any basis to
decide that the decisions of the Board produced as X & Y are erroneous.
In these circumstances, I hold that there is no basis to issue notices on the
Respondents. Thus, the notices are refused, and the application is dismissed
without costs.
Application dismissed. No Costs.
JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL
