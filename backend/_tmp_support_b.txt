FACTS
(1) The Preliminary Plan dated 13.12.2000 was prepared by the Licensed Surveyor.
(2) The learned District Judge delivered judgment on 26.03.2008.
(3) Deeds P2 and P3 dated 07.04.1944 describe the land in the same manner.

LAW
(4) Section 18(1)(a)(iii) of the Partition Law No. 21 of 1977 states that the surveyor shall state whether the land surveyed is substantially the same as the land described in the plaint.

EVIDENCE
(5) The Surveyor's Report states that the surveyed land is the same land described in the schedule to the plaint.

DEFENCE
(6) The Defendant stated that the land has not been properly identified due to the difference in extent.

RELIEF
(7) The Plaintiffs state that each is entitled to a 1/6 share of the land.
