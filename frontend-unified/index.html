LegalAI Sri Lanka

A unified AI-powered platform for Sri Lankan legal research and compliance auditing.

Platform Overview

LegalAI integrates four specialized AI tools built specifically for the Sri Lankan legal system — covering case law research, argument analysis, document processing, and contract compliance.

Technology Stack

  • Flask REST API — Case search & classification (port 5000)
  • FastAPI + Qwen2.5 AI — Argument scoring (port 8000)
  • FastAPI + OCR/Tesseract — Document extraction (port 8001)
  • FastAPI + NLP — Compliance auditing (port 8002)

Data Sources

The platform draws on a dataset of Sri Lankan Supreme Court civil and criminal cases, Sri Lankan civil statutes, and AI models fine-tuned specifically for Sri Lankan legal language.

Research Project

Developed as part of a research initiative at a Sri Lankan university to demonstrate the application of AI/ML to the local legal domain. © 2026 All rights reserved.
