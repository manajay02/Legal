LegDoc

Sri Lanka Court System — AI-Powered Legal Intelligence

Our Mission

LegDoc is an AI-powered legal document analysis platform designed to support Sri Lanka's court system. We aim to make legal information more accessible, searchable, and understandable through cutting-edge natural language processing and machine learning technologies.

What We Do

Similarity Search

Find related legal cases using AI-powered TF-IDF text matching across the Sri Lankan court case database.

Case Classification

Automatically classify legal documents into categories and subcategories using machine learning models.

Case Extraction

Extract key information from legal documents including parties, dates, verdicts, and legal references.

Case Summarization

Generate concise, accurate summaries of lengthy legal documents to save time and improve comprehension.

Technology

Built with Flask, MongoDB, and modern machine learning (SVM classifiers, TF-IDF vectorization, and sentence transformers) to deliver fast, accurate legal document intelligence.

Help & User Guide

Learn how to use LegDoc effectively

1 Getting Started

After logging in, you will land on the Similarity Search page. Use the sidebar on the left to navigate between different tools. Your session stays active until you log out using the button in the top-right corner.

2 Classify & Add a Case

Under the Classify / Add Case tab you can:

  • Upload a PDF — Click “Choose File” and select a legal case PDF document.
  • Paste text — Copy the full text of a legal document into the text area.
  • Click “Classify Document” to let the AI predict its category (Civil / Criminal) and subcategory (e.g. Land Dispute, Murder).
  • Click “Add Case to Database” to save the document permanently. Duplicate detection will prevent re-adding the same file.

3 Search by Filename

Switch to the By Filename tab. Select a Category and Subcategory from the dropdowns. A list of matching cases will appear — click any case to select it, then configure the similarity options and click “Find Similar Cases”.

4 Search by Text

Switch to the By Text tab. Paste or type the text of a legal document and click “Find Similar Cases”. The AI will compare it against the entire database and return the most similar cases ranked by score.

5 Viewing Results

Search results appear as cards showing the case filename, category, subcategory, and a similarity score. Click “View” to open the full case text in a modal overlay.

6 Case Extractor

Navigate to Case Extractor in the sidebar. Upload a PDF or paste text to extract structured information such as parties involved, court details, dates, and legal references from a case.

7 Case Summarizer

Navigate to Case Summarizer in the sidebar. Upload or paste a legal document and the AI will generate a concise summary highlighting the key facts, arguments, and judgment of the case.

Tips & Troubleshooting

  • For best results, use complete case documents rather than excerpts.
  • PDF files should be text-based (not scanned images) for accurate extraction.
  • If the “Add Case” button shows a duplicate warning, the document already exists in the database.
  • Use the “Top N” dropdown to control how many similar results are returned.
  • Enable “Global Search” to search across all categories, or leave it off to search within the selected category only.
